Become a Mentor. Make a lasting impact.

Share your experience, judgment, and perspective with emerging leaders across the Army.

Why mentor through ROCKS?

  • Support the development of emerging Army leaders.
  • Share lessons from command, staff, transition, and civilian leadership.
  • Remain engaged in a national leadership community.
  • Help strengthen character, confidence, and readiness.
